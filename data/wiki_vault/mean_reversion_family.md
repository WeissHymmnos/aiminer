## Definition

Mean reversion strategies buy temporary dislocations that are expected to normalize.

## Typical Inputs

- Short-horizon returns
- VWAP deviation
- Liquidity imbalance
- Residual spreads

## Common Failure Modes

- Structural breaks
- Fundamental repricing
- Liquidity vacuum persistence
