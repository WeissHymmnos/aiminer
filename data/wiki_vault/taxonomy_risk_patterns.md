# Taxonomy: Risk Pattern

## Purpose

This page groups all `risk_pattern` nodes and highlights how they relate to the research graph.

## Nodes

- [[implementation_drift_risk]] **Implementation Drift Risk** · `baseline` / `active` — The implemented expression deviates materially from the economic hypothesis or math formula.
- [[simulation_only_risk]] **Simulation-Only Evidence Risk** · `baseline` / `active` — Results that appear promising but only exist on simulated or fallback metrics.
- [[turnover_explosion_risk]] **Turnover Explosion Risk** · `baseline` / `active` — A strategy's gross alpha is overwhelmed by trading costs due to unstable or excessively reactive signals.
