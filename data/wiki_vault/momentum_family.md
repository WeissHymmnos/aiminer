## Definition

Momentum strategies assume return persistence across time or across assets.

## Typical Inputs

- Multi-horizon returns
- Volume confirmation
- Trend filters
- Regime persistence

## Common Failure Modes

- Violent reversal regimes
- Crowding
- Over-extended trend exhaustion
