# Energy Prices Face Volatility Amid Supply Chain Disruptions

**Date:** 2020-11-27
**Category:** Macroeconomics
**Keywords:** energy, commodities, inflation

## Content

 On November 27, 2020, market analysts noted that global energy markets are experiencing increased price swings due to maintenance at major extraction sites and logistical bottlenecks. market participants are hedging against potential winter shortages.
